Low AMH

Being told you have low AMH can feel overwhelming. But AMH reflects egg quantity, and not egg quality—and many women with low AMH can still conceive naturally with the right changes.

You need to focus on improving egg health, cycle regulation, weight management and nutrition. Many women with low AMH have conceived within months in our program.

PCOD

PCOD is often seen as a barrier to conception, but it doesn’t have to be.

Irregular cycles and delayed ovulation can make things unpredictable, but when the underlying hormonal imbalances are addressed, your body can start to function more regularly and support conception.

We have helped hundreds of women with PCOD conceive without any fertility treatments.

Miscarriages

Experiencing a miscarriage can feel confusing and overwhelming. While 50% of the time, it could be due to a random genetic error.

The other 50%, it is not a random event. Factors like hormone balance, egg and sperm quality, thyroid function, and underlying inflammation can all play a role.

When these are identified and supported, the chances of a healthy pregnancy can improve significantly. Many women with previous miscarriages have gone on to conceive and carry successfully with the right approach.

Sperm metrics

Being told there are issues with sperm count, motility or morphology can feel concerning. These factors reflect how sperm move and function - and can make fertilisation more difficult, but they can be improved.

Things like nutrition, oxidative stress, lifestyle habits, and overall health play a key role. We also need to assess if there are any underlying conditions like infections or varicocele that are causing the low count.

Many couples with these challenges have gone on to conceive naturally in our program.

Weight Concerns

Weight can play a role in fertility—but it’s not just about the number on the scale. Both being underweight or overweight can affect overall reproductive health. Fat cells can disrupt hormonal balance and healthy ovulation as they send their own signals.

By focusing on the right nutrition, movement and training and sustainable lifestyle changes, your body can be better supported for conception. Our couples have lost up to 12kgs in 3 months and seen spontaneous positive pregnancies.

Unexplained Infertility

Being told you have unexplained infertility can make you feel helpless—especially when everything looks “normal.” But often, it means the underlying factors haven’t been fully identified yet.

Subtle issues with egg and sperm quality, timing, hormone balance, or the uterine environment can all play a role. When these are addressed, many couples are able to conceive naturally with the right approach. We have seen the quickest pregnancies with unexplained infertility in our program.
